Installation notes

For Windows 10 or 11 on a 64-bit PC. Download the installer, open it, and follow the setup instructions. This build is not digitally code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may display an “unknown publisher” notice.

Search results depend on provider availability; some providers, including Google, may block automated requests and be skipped.
